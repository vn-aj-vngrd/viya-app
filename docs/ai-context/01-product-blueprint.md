# 01 Product Blueprint

## Product name

Viya

## Product type

AI-powered travel planning, readiness, coordination, live assistance, moments, and post-trip wrap-up companion.

## Core positioning

Viya helps travelers feel ready, guided, and remembered across the entire travel life cycle.

Most travel apps answer:

> Where should I go?

Viya answers:

> Am I ready, what should I do next, what could go wrong, and how do I make this trip smoother?

## Product thesis

Travel is not just destination discovery. Travel is an operations problem. Travelers need to plan, organize, prepare, coordinate, adapt, capture memories, and reflect after the trip.

Viya turns scattered travel planning into a proactive AI-guided operating system.

## Core user problem

Travelers use too many disconnected tools:

- ChatGPT for ideas
- Google Maps for places
- Booking apps for hotels and activities
- Email for confirmations
- Calendar for schedules
- Notes for checklists
- Group chats for decisions
- Screenshots for important details
- Photo apps for memories

The traveler becomes the project manager.

## Primary pain points

### Fragmented planning

Trip information is scattered across many apps, making it hard to create one clear plan.

### No readiness visibility

Travelers cannot easily answer: "Am I actually ready?"

### Passive AI

Most AI travel tools wait for the user to ask. Viya should proactively detect problems and recommend actions.

### Live travel chaos

During the trip, delays, weather, budget issues, timing problems, and group coordination failures happen in real time.

### Lost post-trip value

Photos, memories, budget details, and travel learnings are rarely organized into a useful recap.

## Product vision

Viya helps every traveler move through the full travel life cycle with confidence.

## Travel life cycle

1. Dreaming
2. Planning
3. Booking and organizing
4. Readiness preparation
5. Pre-departure
6. Live travel
7. Group coordination
8. Moments capture
9. Post-trip wrap-up
10. Future trip insights

## Key product pillars

### AI Trip Planner

Generates realistic, personalized, route-aware itineraries based on destination, dates, budget, travel style, group size, and pace.

### Trip Hub

Central source of truth for itinerary, bookings, documents, checklists, risks, reminders, and moments.

### Viya Readiness Score

A preparedness score that checks bookings, documents, packing, budget, schedule, weather, transportation, safety, and group readiness.

### Fix My Readiness

Turns readiness gaps into action. Creates tasks, reminders, backup plans, and suggested itinerary changes.

### Risk Radar

Proactively detects travel risks such as weather issues, missing bookings, unrealistic schedules, budget pressure, and group blockers.

### TripSync

Keeps group travelers aligned with shared itinerary, tasks, voting, decisions, and readiness status.

### Travel Mode

A live travel assistant for the current day with next activity, quick actions, booking access, replan tools, and proactive alerts.

### Moments

Captures trip highlights, photos, notes, captions, favorite places, and daily summaries.

### Viya Wrapped

Post-trip AI-generated recap with best moments, stats, travel personality, budget summary, and shareable cards.

### Viya Insights

Learns from past trips and improves future planning recommendations.

## Target users

### Busy professionals

Want efficient trip planning, readiness, and reminders.

### Group travelers

Need coordination, decisions, shared checklists, and reminders.

### First-time or occasional travelers

Need confidence, structure, and step-by-step help.

### Experience-driven travelers

Want beautiful memories, captions, shareable recaps, and travel insights.

## MVP promise

In the MVP, users should be able to:

1. Create a trip.
2. Generate a realistic itinerary.
3. View a trip dashboard.
4. See their readiness score.
5. Fix missing readiness items.
6. Detect major risks.
7. Use Travel Mode for the current day.
8. Save moments.
9. Generate Viya Wrapped.

## Winning demo message

Viya is not another itinerary generator. Viya is an AI travel operations companion that helps travelers prepare, adapt, coordinate, and remember the trip.
