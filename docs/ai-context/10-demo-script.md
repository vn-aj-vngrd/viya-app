# 10 Demo Script

## Purpose

This file defines the hackathon/product demo story for Viya.

The demo should show that Viya is more than an AI itinerary generator. It should show Viya as a proactive travel operations companion.

## Demo trip

Destination: Palawan, Philippines
Dates: June 10–15
Arrival airport: Puerto Princesa
Arrival time: 4:35 PM
Departure time: 5:15 PM
Travel style: Balanced, scenic, not too tiring
Group: Couple or small friend group
Budget: Mid-range

## Demo story

The user wants to plan a Palawan trip but feels unsure if everything is ready.

Viya helps them:

1. Create the trip.
2. Generate a realistic itinerary.
3. Detect planning gaps.
4. Improve readiness.
5. Monitor risks.
6. Assist during travel.
7. Capture moments.
8. Generate a post-trip recap.

## Demo flow

### Step 1: Welcome

Show the landing screen.

Message:

> Meet Viya, your AI travel operations companion.

Primary action:

> Plan your first trip

### Step 2: Trip onboarding

Enter:

- Palawan
- June 10–15
- Arrival 4:35 PM
- Departure 5:15 PM
- Balanced travel style
- Mid-range budget

Viya asks smart questions:

- Do you prefer a relaxed or packed trip?
- Are you staying in Puerto Princesa, El Nido, or both?
- Do you want beach, food, adventure, or rest?

### Step 3: AI itinerary generation

Show loading states:

- Building itinerary
- Checking travel time
- Creating readiness checklist
- Detecting risks

### Step 4: Trip dashboard

Show generated dashboard:

- Readiness Score: 68%
- Next action: Fix missing bookings
- Risk alert: Arrival day is late
- Itinerary preview
- Checklist preview

### Step 5: Viya detects gaps

Example gaps:

- Missing accommodation for final nights
- No airport transfer
- Day 2 is overloaded
- Weather risk on island hopping day
- Packing checklist incomplete

### Step 6: Fix My Readiness

User taps `Fix My Readiness`.

Viya generates:

- Airport transfer task
- Hotel booking reminder
- Rain backup plan
- Packing checklist
- Budget estimate

Readiness improves from 68% to 84%.

### Step 7: Risk Radar

Show risks:

- High: Rain likely on Day 3
- Medium: Long transfer time
- Medium: Missing hotel confirmation

User taps action:

> Move island hopping earlier

### Step 8: Travel Mode

Show current day.

Viya says:

> You arrived late. I adjusted tonight’s plan and moved dinner closer to your hotel.

Quick actions:

- Replan today
- Show booking
- Find nearby food
- Send group update

### Step 9: Moments

User saves a moment from island hopping.

Viya generates caption:

> Blue water, slow mornings, and the kind of view worth planning for.

### Step 10: Viya Wrapped

Show post-trip recap:

- Travel personality: Island Explorer
- 12 places visited
- Most memorable day: Day 3
- Top moment: Island hopping
- Budget summary
- Next trip suggestion: Siargao or Bohol

## Winning pitch line

Viya is not another app that tells you where to go. Viya helps you become ready, stay guided, handle changes, and remember the trip afterward.

## Demo success criteria

The judge should understand:

- The problem is real and painful.
- The AI is proactive, not passive.
- The product covers the full travel life cycle.
- The readiness score is a unique hook.
- The app is visually premium and easy to understand.
- Viya can become market viable beyond the hackathon.
