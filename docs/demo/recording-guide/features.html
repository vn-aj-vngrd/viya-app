Features guide

Viya turns a trip into a ready workspace.

A visual guide to the app flow: sign in, onboard, create a trip, generate modules, upload proof, and finish at readiness.

Core idea Viya is a trip workspace with an AI assistant inside it. The assistant can ask, generate, adjust, audit, and stage changes across itinerary, Vault, budget, and readiness.

Get started and login

The app starts with account-backed trip context.

Use the simulator flow: Get started, Continue with Google, Continue on the Google permission prompt, then choose the saved default account.

Onboarding and Home

Viya collects defaults before planning starts.

Onboarding gives Viya reusable travel preferences. Home then becomes the dashboard for suggestions, active trips, and the next planning action.

  • Onboarding questionsTrip vibe, pace, budget, interests, food, companions, suggestion style, and rest needs.
  • Home tourDashboard context, trip suggestions, active trip entry points, and path into the Trips tab.
  • AI setup valueThe assistant can use preferences as defaults instead of asking the same questions every time.

Generate itinerary

AI creates a plan inside the itinerary module.

Use the itinerary Viya action to generate the schedule from the Singapore trip context, pacing, budget, and requirements.

Prompt backup: Generate a realistic Singapore itinerary for this work-leisure group trip. Keep the pacing balanced, add buffers, keep evenings light, and include one small victory-lap moment.
What to show The output should be multi-day, paced, and connected to trip requirements. Do not present a generic attraction list as the final state.

Upload files in Vault

Vault turns trip files into workspace evidence.

Start from the empty Vault screen, then upload the fixture documents. TripSync reviews extracted details before linking them to bookings, budget, and readiness.

AI integration Extraction is reviewable. Viya should use evidence from files, not invent confirmed bookings or costs.

Budget and readiness

Spend and preparedness update from evidence.

The live starter workspace begins with the PHP planning ceiling and no expenses. After Vault evidence is reviewed, Budget and Readiness can be synced from proof.

  • Budget AISyncs confirmed flight, hotel, and receipt evidence against the PHP 90,000 ceiling.
  • Readiness AIAudits documents, bookings, budget, buffers, safety, and schedule load.
  • Finish lineThe prepared state should become visible in the module, not just described by chat.

Ask Viya in the trip

End by asking questions from the completed workspace.

After the modules are connected, Ask Viya should answer from itinerary, Vault, budget, and readiness context.

Question 1: What is the smartest next thing to check before departure, and what evidence are you using from the workspace?
Question 2: Once readiness is complete, what should we watch on travel day so the schedule stays light and the budget stays on track?
End frame Finish on the connected trip workspace overview or the readiness module after checks are complete.
