**Product:** Viya  
**Positioning:** AI Travel Command Center  
**Tagline:** Every trip, beautifully organized.  
**Promise:** Plan in Viya. Book anywhere. Ask Viya anytime. Let Viya organize everything.  
**Prepared for:** Roadmap 01 · New Product Idea Challenge  
**Contest format:** Solo build · 5-week challenge · Week 3 submission lock  

## Product Thesis

Viya is not another itinerary generator and not just a booking organizer. Viya is an AI collaboration layer for the entire travel lifecycle.

A traveler can start with a vague idea, collaborate with AI to shape the trip, create a structured Trip Blueprint, book anywhere, sync confirmations into the workspace, run preparation and readiness checks, receive active travel guidance, track budget, capture memories, and turn the final trip into a shareable story or reusable guide.

The winning point is not one isolated AI feature. The winning point is continuity: Viya keeps context from planning to preparation, travel, budget tracking, memory capture, and post-trip learning.

## 1. Executive Summary

### One-liner

Viya is an AI travel companion that collaborates with travelers across the full trip lifecycle: planning, booking preparation, confirmation syncing, readiness checking, travel guidance, budget coaching, memory capture, and story sharing.

### Core Problem

Travel planning is fragmented. A single trip often lives across booking apps, email confirmations, screenshots, receipts, notes, calendars, Google Maps, chats, and spreadsheets. This creates stress, missed requirements, budget surprises, scattered memories, and poor continuity between planning and the actual trip.

Most AI travel tools stop at itinerary generation. Most travel apps focus on booking or static organization. The traveler still has to connect the dots manually.

### Core Solution

Viya consolidates the full journey into one mobile-first AI workspace. It combines natural planning, trip blueprints, booking guidance, confirmation extraction, trip vault, readiness and compliance checks, itinerary calendar, travel mode, budget wallet, memory capture, Viya Insights, and trip story generation.

The product experience is built around an AI collaboration loop:

1. **Ask** - the user starts with a natural travel goal or question.
2. **Shape** - Viya asks follow-up questions and turns vague intent into structured options.
3. **Decide** - the user reviews, edits, accepts, or rejects recommendations.
4. **Sync** - Viya extracts real-world confirmations, receipts, and documents into the trip workspace.
5. **Audit** - Viya checks readiness, budget, schedule, and missing requirements.
6. **Guide** - Viya provides Now / Next / Watch out travel guidance.
7. **Learn** - Viya summarizes what happened, what changed, and what to improve next time.

## 3. Target Users and UX Principles

### Primary Users

- **Budget-conscious travelers:** users who want to stay within a specific spend cap.
- **First-time travelers:** users who need help with passports, visas, airport requirements, documents, and what to bring.
- **Young professionals, couples, and barkadas:** users who want trips to feel easy, social, and organized.
- **Frequent local travelers:** users who want reusable trip templates and real-world guides.
- **Travel sharers:** users who want beautiful recaps, story templates, and shareable guides.

### UX Principles

- **Design for zero confusion:** every screen should answer what is happening, what is done, what is missing, and what to do next.
- **Conversation plus visuals:** Viya chats while the screen shows tappable answers, cards, budget ranges, warnings, and next actions.
- **User stays in control:** Viya suggests and explains, but the user confirms plans, bookings, changes, and shared content.
- **Proactive but not annoying:** alerts should be useful, contextual, and adjustable by user preference.
- **Continuity over isolated AI:** each AI action should update the trip workspace and improve future guidance.
- **Light, calm, mobile-first experience:** the app should feel focused, friendly, polished, and easy to demo.

## 4. End-to-End Product Flow

### Lifecycle

**Plan -> Book -> Sync -> Check -> Travel -> Track -> Capture -> Share**

### Flow Details

1. **Planning Mode**  
   The user chats with Viya about a travel goal, dream, mood, destination, budget, or timing. Viya asks focused follow-up questions and shows visual options.

2. **Trip Agreement**  
   Once the user and Viya align, Viya generates a Trip Blueprint with destination, route, dates, budget allocation, itinerary style, booking targets, requirements, risks, and next actions.

3. **Booking Phase**  
   The user books externally through preferred apps or sites. Viya may provide booking targets, suggested providers, and deep-link placeholders, but Viya does not process payments in the MVP.

4. **TripSync and Vault**  
   The user uploads or pastes confirmations, receipts, documents, or screenshots. Viya extracts key details and stores files in the trip vault. The user reviews and confirms before anything is saved.

5. **Preparation Readiness**  
   Viya checks if the trip has required bookings, documents, destination requirements, budget buffer, packing items, safety notes, schedule buffers, and emergency contact coverage.

6. **Travel Mode**  
   During the trip, Viya uses confirmed trip data to provide Now / Next / Watch out guidance, reminders, alerts, document lookup, and ad-hoc answers.

7. **Budget Tracking**  
   Booked costs and logged expenses deduct from planned budget and pocket money. Viya warns when spending is too fast, suggests adjustments, or identifies extra room for upgrades.

8. **Memory Capture**  
   Users attach photos, receipts, notes, ratings, and tips to itinerary events. Viya prompts at useful moments without interrupting the trip.

9. **Trip Story and Community Template**  
   After or during the trip, Viya generates shareable posts, daily story cards, a Travel Wrapped-style recap, slides, and an optional public guide that other users can copy and adapt.

## 6. AI Collaboration Across the Full Trip Cycle

Viya should be judged as an AI-native product because AI is not limited to a single extraction or chat feature. AI creates a connected loop across planning, preparation, travel, and recap.

### 6.1 Planning Collaboration

Viya starts with a natural conversation and helps the user make decisions.

AI responsibilities:

- Interpret vague travel intent.
- Ask follow-up questions only when needed.
- Compare destination options against budget, pace, season, duration, and travel style.
- Explain trade-offs in plain language.
- Generate a structured Trip Blueprint.
- Let the user accept, edit, or regenerate the plan.

Demo proof:

- User enters one natural prompt.
- Viya asks focused follow-ups.
- Viya presents multiple destination options with fit score and budget risk.
- User chooses a plan and generates the Trip Blueprint.

### 6.2 Preparation and TripSync Intelligence

TripSync is the most visual proof point, but it is part of a broader preparation system.

AI responsibilities:

- Extract booking data from confirmations, screenshots, PDFs, receipts, and pasted text.
- Identify booking type, provider, amount, dates, times, location, reference number, passengers, and missing fields.
- Ask the user to review extracted details before saving.
- Update itinerary, budget, vault metadata, checklist, readiness score, and travel mode.
- Detect what remains missing after each sync.

Demo proof:

- Upload a transport confirmation.
- Review extracted details.
- Confirm sync.
- Watch multiple modules update automatically.

### 6.3 Readiness and Compliance Intelligence

Viya continuously audits whether the traveler is actually ready.

AI responsibilities:

- Check missing bookings, documents, emergency contacts, packing items, budget buffer, and schedule gaps.
- Explain readiness score changes.
- Recommend the next best action.
- Prioritize urgent items over nice-to-have items.
- Provide guidance without claiming official legal authority for travel requirements.

Demo proof:

- Readiness score changes after TripSync.
- Viya identifies missing hotel confirmation, transfer, packing list, and emergency contact.
- User asks “What is missing?” and receives evidence-backed insight cards.

### 6.4 Travel Mode Intelligence

During travel, Viya becomes a lightweight command center.

AI responsibilities:

- Show Now / Next / Watch out using confirmed trip data.
- Answer questions about booking references, check-in times, remaining budget, next activity, and document location.
- Suggest adjustments when the schedule is too packed, delayed, expensive, or risky.
- Surface contextual alerts at the right time.

Demo proof:

- Travel Mode shows the current ferry event, next lunch plan, budget today, and watch-out gap before hotel check-in.
- User asks “What should I do during the gap?” and Viya gives useful options.

### 6.5 Budget Intelligence

Viya turns budget from a static estimate into a living travel coach.

AI responsibilities:

- Deduct booked costs after confirmation sync.
- Track manual expenses, receipt uploads, and event-linked costs.
- Compare actual spend against daily and category budgets.
- Warn when spending is too fast.
- Suggest trade-offs to protect the trip goal.

Demo proof:

- Synced ferry booking deducts from transport budget.
- User logs brunch expense.
- Viya explains remaining daily budget and dinner recommendation.

### 6.6 Memory and Story Intelligence

Viya turns trip data into memories and reusable knowledge.

AI responsibilities:

- Attach notes, photos, receipts, ratings, and tips to itinerary events.
- Summarize actual spend, best moments, hidden costs, and lessons learned.
- Generate shareable captions, story slides, Travel Wrapped-style cards, and optional copyable guide.
- Keep private data private unless the user chooses to share.

Demo proof:

- User saves a memory to an itinerary event.
- Viya generates a trip story with budget summary, best moments, tips, and shareable guide.

## 7. Killer Features

### 7.1 Viya Live Planner

Start with any travel idea. Users can type, speak later, tap quick replies, or edit fields manually. Viya asks just enough questions to create a useful plan without forcing users into a rigid form.

- Mood-first, budget-first, date-first, destination-first, occasion-first, and open-ended planning.
- Screen shows known details, missing details, quick replies, destination cards, budget ranges, and warnings.
- Output is a Trip Blueprint the user can accept, adjust, or regenerate.

### 7.2 Trip Blueprint

Viya converts natural planning into a structured agreement.

- Destination, route, dates, travelers, budget, pace, and style.
- Budget allocation and estimated cost range.
- Booking targets and preparation requirements.
- Risks, trade-offs, and next actions.
- Creates the initial trip workspace after user approval.

### 7.3 TripSync

Upload any booking confirmation and Viya syncs the details into the trip workspace.

- Extracts booking type, provider, dates, times, amount, reference number, guests/passengers, meeting point, cancellation policy, and missing fields.
- Updates itinerary calendar, budget wallet, smart checklist, vault metadata, travel mode, and readiness score.
- Always uses an extraction review screen so users confirm or edit before saving.

### 7.4 Readiness and Compliance Check

Viya audits whether the user is actually ready to travel.

- Checks bookings, documents, passport/visa guidance, IDs, airport fees, eSIM/roaming, insurance, packing, budget buffer, and emergency contacts.
- Generates a Trip Readiness Score and next best action.
- Especially valuable for first-time and international travelers.

### 7.5 Itinerary Calendar and Alerts

Viya turns the agreed trip plan and synced confirmations into a scheduled calendar.

- Supports timeline view, day planner, manual edits, travel buffers, event budgets, checklist items, and event memory slots.
- Sends contextual push notification simulations for upcoming events, required items, budget warnings, and memory prompts.
- Detects conflicts such as an activity scheduled before arrival or a long gap before hotel check-in.

### 7.6 Travel Mode

A live, minimal mode for active travel.

- Shows Now, Next, Watch out, budget today, reminders, and documents.
- Answers ad-hoc questions such as hotel check-out, remaining budget, booking reference, or next planned activity.
- Allows dynamic itinerary changes: make it more relaxed, more adventurous, cheaper, or slower.

### 7.7 Budget Wallet and Pocket Money

Viya tracks real travel spending against the user’s agreed budget.

- Booked costs are deducted after TripSync confirmation.
- Pocket money and daily budget are tracked during travel.
- Users can log expenses by text, voice later, receipt upload, or itinerary event.
- Viya gives spending insights and adjustment recommendations.

### 7.8 Viya Insights

Ask Viya anything at any phase of the trip. Viya Insights turns trip data into clear answers, summaries, risks, recommendations, and post-trip learnings.

- Planning insights: explain why a destination fits or is risky based on budget, pace, season, requirements, and travel style.
- Booking insights: summarize confirmations, missing fields, cancellation notes, and budget impact.
- Readiness insights: answer what is missing, what is urgent, and what should be done next before travel.
- Travel insights: answer ad-hoc questions about check-out, next activity, remaining budget, document location, and itinerary changes.
- Post-trip insights: generate spend summaries, hidden costs, best moments, lessons learned, and recommendations for the next trip.
- Community insights: compare a user’s plan against shared trips and identify realistic budget ranges or useful traveler tips.

### 7.9 Viya Moments and Trip Story

Viya turns a trip into a personal memory and optional public guide.

- Users attach photos, receipts, notes, ratings, and tips to itinerary events.
- Viya generates shareable posts, daily story cards, carousel templates, and an end-of-trip Travel Wrapped-style recap.
- Users can keep the output private, export it as a memory slideshow, or share it publicly with subtle Viya attribution and a copyable trip guide.

## 10. Technical Architecture

| Layer | Selected Stack / Service |
|---|---|
| Mobile Frontend | Expo React Native |
| Backend-as-a-Service | Convex |
| Database | Convex Database with TypeScript schema, queries, mutations, and reactive client updates |
| Authentication | Convex Auth for MVP; Clerk + Convex can be used later if social login polish is needed |
| File Storage / Viya Vault | Convex File Storage for trip documents, booking confirmations, receipts, IDs, and memory photos; storage IDs are linked to trip records |
| Realtime | Convex reactive queries for live trip dashboard updates, budget changes, checklist changes, itinerary state, and vault metadata |
| AI Orchestration | Vercel AI SDK for model abstraction and structured generation |
| AI Providers | Claude preferred; OpenAI/Gemini/OpenRouter configurable for demo fallback |
| Vision Extraction | Vision-capable AI model to read screenshots/images/PDF-rendered pages and return structured JSON |
| Text-to-Speech | Optional simulated speak mode for demo; Google Text-to-Speech can be used later |
| Push Notifications | Expo Notifications for itinerary reminders, readiness alerts, and budget warnings |
| Backend Logic | Convex functions: queries/mutations for app state; actions/HTTP actions for AI calls, file processing, TripSync extraction, and notification jobs |

### High-Level Data Flow

1. User creates a trip or chats with Viya.
2. The app sends planning context to an AI route using Vercel AI SDK.
3. AI returns a structured Trip Blueprint saved to Convex Database through a mutation.
4. User uploads confirmations, receipts, photos, or PDFs to Convex File Storage.
5. The returned storage IDs and metadata are linked to the trip, booking, itinerary event, or memory moment.
6. AI extraction route processes the file or text and returns structured booking/expense data.
7. User confirms or edits extraction.
8. Confirmed records update bookings, itinerary items, budget entries, checklist items, readiness score, vault metadata, and travel mode.
9. Convex reactive queries update the mobile app in real time.
10. Expo Notifications triggers itinerary alerts, Viya Autopilot reminders, readiness nudges, and memory prompts.

## 12. AI Output Contracts

### Trip Blueprint JSON

AI responses should be structured and validated before use. Example fields: title, destination, origin, travelers, budget, trip style, fit score, budget status, estimated cost range, budget allocation, why this fits, risks, next actions.

### Booking Extraction JSON

Example fields: documentType, provider, title, bookingReference, amount, currency, startDateTime, endDateTime, location, participants, confidence, missingFields, rawSummary.

### Autopilot Audit JSON

Example fields: tripStatus, readinessScore, issues, budgetInsight, scheduleInsight, nextBestAction. Autopilot should run after meaningful state changes: new booking, new expense, itinerary edit, trip date approaching, or checklist completion.

### Viya Insights JSON

Example fields: phase, userQuestion, answer, evidence, relatedBookings, relatedExpenses, relatedItineraryItems, riskLevel, recommendations, nextBestAction, summaryCards.

### Trip Story JSON

Example fields: title, actualSpend, plannedBudget, budgetStatus, highlights, hiddenCosts, tips, recommendedBudget, slides, socialCaption, shareVisibility, wrappedStats, shareCards, communityGuideSummary.

## 17. Risks, Guardrails, and Trust

- **AI extraction errors:** Always show a review screen with confidence score and manual edit before saving.
- **Outdated travel requirements:** Label requirements as guidance and encourage checking official sources; later integrate official requirement APIs or curated sources.
- **Notification fatigue:** Allow proactivity levels: Minimal, Standard, Hands-on.
- **Privacy:** Trips and files are private by default; users explicitly choose what to share.
- **Sensitive documents:** Store vault files with user-scoped access and signed URLs; never expose public buckets for private files.
- **Booking liability:** MVP uses external booking links and confirmation import; Viya does not process payments or issue tickets.
- **AI overreach:** AI suggestions should be explainable, reviewable, and reversible.

## 18. Final Product Summary

### Final Positioning

Viya is a complete AI travel command center. Start with any travel idea, collaborate naturally with Viya, book anywhere, sync confirmations into the vault, complete readiness and compliance checks, follow a proactive travel mode, track budget and pocket money, capture memories by itinerary event, and turn the trip into a beautiful private memory or shareable guide.

### Final Winning Statement

Viya wins because it shows what AI products should become: not a one-shot generator, but a persistent collaborator that understands context, updates the workspace, guides decisions, and stays useful across the entire user journey.
